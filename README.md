A working knowledge base for **using AI in retail design, visual merchandising, and store branding**. It is not a link dump. It combines three things:

1. **Retail design knowledge:** planning, flow, lighting, materials, and VM principles, so you can judge what AI gives you.
2. **AI practice:** verified tool reviews, a prompt library, step-by-step workflows, quality checks, and legal guidance.
3. **Templates:** questionnaires, briefs, audits, BOQ and finish schedules you can use on Monday.

No coding needed.

## How this repo stays honest

- **Tool pages carry a review date** and separate vendor claims from practical limits. Tools change fast. Open an issue when something is out of date.
- **Prompts are labelled Starter or Field-tested.** Starter means written from design experience but not yet proven in every tool.
- **Numbers are rules of thumb**, marked as such. Codes and standards come first.
- **Case studies are real or absent.** None are invented.
- **AI is a tool, not an authority.** Drawings, suppliers, and qualified professionals confirm everything that gets built.
