# Contributing

Thanks for helping. You don't need to code. Everything here is text, and you can edit it in your browser.

## Quickest way: open an issue

- [Suggest a tool](../../issues/new?template=add-a-tool.yml)
- [Share a prompt](../../issues/new?template=add-a-prompt.yml)
- [Report outdated information](../../issues/new?template=report-outdated.yml)

## Send a pull request from your browser

1. Open the file and click the pencil icon (**Edit this file**).
2. Change it, then click **Commit changes** and **Propose changes**.
3. Click **Create pull request**.

## Standards

### Facts and tools
- Link to the **official source** for any claim about features, models, or terms.
- Say what the tool does for retail design, and where it falls short.
- Don't state prices. Say "subscription", "credits", or "free tier".
- Put a **Reviewed: Month Year** line on tool pages, and update it when you re-check.
- No affiliate links.

### Prompts
1. Copy [`prompts/_TEMPLATE.md`](prompts/_TEMPLATE.md) into the right stage folder.
2. Fill in every section, including **What to check** and **Common problems**.
3. Add it to the [prompt index](prompts/README.md).
4. Mark it **Field-tested** only if you ran it on a real project, and add your tool, version, and date.

### Guides
- Mark rules of thumb as rules of thumb, and point to codes and standards.
- Keep language plain. Define terms in the [glossary](guides/glossary.md).

### Case studies
Use the [template](case-studies/_TEMPLATE.md). Real projects only. Remove client names unless you have permission. Label AI-generated images. Report only numbers you measured.

## What we won't accept

- Tools that generate real people's likeness or imitate a brand's work without rights
- Prompts built to copy a named living artist or a competitor's store
- Unverified or promotional claims such as "best AI tool ever"
- Content you don't have the right to share

By contributing you agree to follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
